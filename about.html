About myexxchampagne

myexxchampagne is the artist name of Amir Kayon Campbell-Walker, a recording artist, mix engineer, and writer from Milwaukee, Wisconsin, based in Arizona. His music makes room for melancholy, deep desire, and the parts of a story that usually stay unsaid.

The music

For the parts of a story that usually stay unsaid.

The songs are personal, but they are not trying to give every feeling a neat ending. They follow melancholy, deep unfulfilled desires, and the experiences that resist easy answers.

The goal is to make honest work, built with intention and allowed to become what it needs to be.
